    Prepare a client review package¶

    User guide

    Outcome: Deliver a decision brief, attributed review, technical appendix, unchanged evidence and instructions for independent recipient verification.

    Audience: Evaluation engineers, client reviewers and release owners.

    Prerequisites: Python 3.12 or newer, a compatible InvarLock core, an existing evidence directory and a separately authored review context.

    The package explains a specific baseline-to-candidate change. It does not infer business suitability from a passing policy result or turn commentary into an approval. Native, captured, judge and combined evidence use their existing report validation. Packaging performs no model calls. Optional independent receipt expectations enable an authenticated snapshot; combined snapshots use the existing component recheck.

    Supply the context¶

    Start from the context example and replace its illustrative text. The JSON format is invarlock/client-review-context-v1:

    Field Meaning
    format, title Format identifier and client-facing review title
    review_date, byline Date as YYYY-MM-DD, and author or accountable team
    context Nonempty list of two-string rows: label and explanation
    next_action Named follow-up, owner and timing
    label Optional attribution label; defaults to authored client review
    recommendation, decision Optional owner statements; absent decisions stay explicitly pending
    basis Optional labeled rationale rows, distinct from recorded measurements
    summary Optional plain-language summary: up to four labeled rows, each string at most 600 characters

    Include intended use, changed inputs, business purpose, policy rationale, workload coverage and decision ownership. Do not invent a sample-size rationale or claim that an illustrative workload represents production. Commentary is plain text. Unknown fields, duplicate keys and invalid dates are rejected. The context is limited to 64 KiB, each text field to 4,000 characters and each row list to 32. The shorter summary limits above are an exception. Existing contexts without this optional field remain supported; their briefs explain the recorded scores, change range and requirements and explicitly mark the missing application-specific interpretation. The generated reading guide does not supply a business decision.

    Write the summary for a reader who does not know the metric or statistical method. Use four rows: What was tested, What happened, What this means, and What remains unknown. Describe the task and case count, explain the score in ordinary words, and state the requirement and its outcome. Distinguish an observed decline from uncertainty that prevents a pass. A passing comparison does not by itself establish improvement or application suitability. Keep the intended change in context, and the accountable next step in next_action.

    The summary is attributed commentary, not automatically verified against the measurements. Its author must check numerical statements and limits against the retained evidence. It appears in both the brief and full review; exact measurements, methods and all recorded limitations remain in the full review and appendix. For concrete examples, add labeled basis rows citing retained record paths and JSON pointers. Explain how examples were selected and show counterexamples when available; do not use an illustration to imply a frequency or a general capability.

    Build a fresh package¶

    invarlock report ./evidence \
  --review-context ./review-context.json \
  --package ./client-handoff \
  --retained-receipt ./historical-verification.receipt.json \
  --json

    Omit --retained-receipt when no historical receipt is supplied. Its evidence reference must match, but packaging does not authenticate the receipt's signature, authority or claimed result. It remains visibly labeled an unverified attachment.

    The output directory must be new and outside the evidence. Symlinks and special files are rejected. Evidence copies are bounded to 2 GiB, 4,096 entries including directories and the root, and 16 nested directory levels. A source change during preparation aborts publication. Individual report flags and case selection cannot be combined with package mode.

    Open client-handoff/brief.html. Deliver the directory or its handoff.zip:

    File Purpose
    brief.html, brief.md Concise owner decision, recorded result, key values, limits and next action
    review.html, review.md Full attributed context and rationale with technical results
    technical-report.html, .md Evidence-only rendering with all retained metric results
    verification.html, .md Package-local verification steps and troubleshooting
    review-context.json Exact supplied commentary bytes
    evidence/ Every original evidence file, copied byte for byte
    verification.receipt.json Optional unchanged historical receipt
    original-file-hashes.json SHA-256 identities of copied originals
    package-manifest.json Generator identity and hashes of delivered files, excluding itself and the ZIP

    The brief preserves full conditions and all blockers; omitted passing results are counted and remain available in the appendix. HTML uses local links and no remote assets. Print the brief for a short decision record and keep the full review alongside it. The supported PDF exporter prints evidence and snapshot identities and explicitly requires the companion package for supporting records and replay.

    Each displayed result links directly to its evidence and checks in the technical appendix. Score bars start at zero. Accuracy uses the full 0–100% scale; judge ratings use their 0–1 rubric and are explicitly not accuracy percentages. Loss comparisons use the recorded NLL units and label their shared scale. Custom metrics and missing or unsupported values use text without an inferred scale. The observed scores and the uncertainty range have different roles: the linked checks retain the exact rule that produced the recorded decision.

    Unmet checks have suggested follow-up actions, such as resolving missing results, reviewing uncertainty or investigating candidate behavior. These suggestions do not change the policy, authorize another run or replace the author's next action. The brief and full review retain family-specific limits. Unsigned inventories identify bytes relative to an inventory; they do not establish signer authority or client acceptance. The delivery exporter creates a separate inventory and archive covering the PDFs; it preserves the original package inventory unchanged.

    Recipient verification and decision¶

    Extract the ZIP, read verification.html, and obtain approved trust inputs from an independent source. Keep trust profiles and private verifier keys outside the package. The guide supplies commands for its evidence family and writes a new result and receipt to a fresh external directory. A historical receipt or a public key in a submitted package is not an independent trust anchor.

    A successful scoped verification is separate from the owner's release decision. An adverse result can still be authenticated evidence. Inspect integrity, replay and policy outcomes in the result rather than treating every nonzero exit as the same failure. Evidence-set results preserve the component receipts' distinct scopes; their aggregate result is not a new signed attestation.

    Retain the original evidence, context, review, new verification result and receipt, approved input identities and separately recorded owner decision. For full trust semantics, see evidence and verification and reports and receipts.

    Structured decisions and generated facts¶

    The optional invarlock/client-review-context-v2 format adds required plain-text intended_use, excluded_uses, decision_conditions and decision_state fields. Keep the other v1 required fields. Use explicit text such as “None declared” when no exclusion or condition is supplied; an empty string is rejected. Decision state is pending, accepted, rejected, conditional or exception. An exception requires exception_rationale. Acceptance or conditional acceptance alongside an unmet or unavailable recorded policy also requires that rationale and is displayed as an owner exception. It never changes a recorded requirement or verdict.

    Version 1 inputs remain supported and their original bytes are retained. They are labelled legacy unstructured commentary: arbitrary decision prose is never parsed into an approval state. A byline identifies the supplied attribution; it does not authenticate authority. Unknown fields, states and forged verification fields are rejected. All conditions remain visible without excerpts, including long inputs.

    All package formats share generated result, authentication, quality-requirement and family-limit facts. Authored interpretation remains separate and may be incorrect; it cannot override those facts. Missing observation and verification times remain unavailable. A review date does not establish evidence freshness.

    Authenticate a receipt snapshot¶

    Add --receipt-expectations /absolute/independent-inputs.json to package mode to check the attached receipt. The closed JSON object has format equal to invarlock/report-verification-inputs-v1, kind equal to runtime, captured, judge or evidence_set, and an expectations object. Obtain this file through an independent trusted channel; never populate it by trusting the submission.

    For native (runtime) and captured evidence, expectations are the keyword arguments of verify_signed_verification_receipt: policy_path, expected_pack_signer_fingerprint, expected_verifier_identity and expected_verifier_fingerprint, plus family bindings. Native requires expected_artifact_digests, expected_schedule_digest and expected_runtime_digests; captured requires expected_run_digests and expected_request_digest. Supply expected_request_digest for native v2 and expected_trust_profile_digest when the receipt was issued with a profile. Omitting the latter expects no recorded profile, rather than disabling checking.

    Judge expectations contain recipient_policy_path, expected_verifier_identity and expected_verifier_fingerprint. The independently supplied policy's canonical identity must match the authenticated receipt, which must name this exact evidence. Combined expectations contain recipient_policy_path; the existing combined verifier freshly rechecks both components and compares the complete stored result. Its independent profiles must provide the existing verifier signing configuration. This is not a new signed joint attestation. Keep private keys outside the package. Relative policy paths resolve from the expectations file's directory.

    verification-snapshot.json records exact evidence, receipt and independent-input identities, verifier identity, receipt authentication time and acceptance separately. The snapshot excludes local policy paths and private keys. The original verification time remains unavailable where the receipt contract has no timestamp. Historical acceptance does not establish current signer authorization or evidence freshness. Changing evidence, policy or expectations requires a new check and package.

    An authentic rejection is rendered as a rejection. A wrong binding, unauthorized verifier or corrupt receipt aborts publication with exit 2. Successful rendering still exits 0 for both accepted and rejected snapshots; use recipient_verification in the JSON output to distinguish them. Without expectations, a supplied receipt remains an unverified attachment. Invalid evidence is rejected rather than given an apparently valid report. This workflow performs no model inference.

    Reading layers and case selection¶

    The brief begins with separate panels for the recorded policy result, evidence signing, recipient verification and owner decision. The comparison and requirements follow, with the owner's full conditions in a distinct section. Supporting facts use labelled rows, and full evidence and snapshot identities remain visible at the end. The pine, cream, teal and copper palette follows the existing product style. Detailed score cards and family limitations precede the attributed narrative. Long valid context continues onto further pages; one or two pages is a target, never a truncation rule.

    The engineering review includes captured-case explanations selected by stable ID: one improved, regressed, unchanged and missing example per overall metric when per-case scores are retained. Otherwise it shows output changed, unchanged and missing categories without scoring again or inferring a quality direction. Category counts cover the retained paired run. They are not added across overlapping slices. Examples show scoring outcomes and record IDs without repeating raw inputs or outputs. Missing categories are stated explicitly. Attempted and excluded totals outside the retained runs remain unknown. Other evidence families retain their own paired-count and independent-unit explanations and case detail where supported; judge repetitions are never counted as additional independent cases. Authenticated operational observations remain supplementary and do not change the policy requirements.

    Export a portable PDF delivery¶

    Install Playwright and its supported Chromium locally, then use the public API:

    from pathlib import Path
from invarlock.engine import export_client_review

export_client_review(
    Path("client-handoff"),
    output=Path("delivery"),
    browser_module=Path("/absolute/node_modules/playwright"),
    paper="A4",  # or Letter
)

    The source-checkout equivalent is python examples/client-review/export.py client-handoff delivery --browser-module /absolute/node_modules/playwright. INVARLOCK_REPORT_BROWSER_EXECUTABLE optionally selects an approved installed browser. The exporter validates the package inventory and writes into a fresh staged directory. A missing browser, stale inventory or interrupted export cannot publish a completed delivery. It never modifies the source package or evidence.

    Deliver delivery.zip for the full extracted offline package, or summary.zip for the brief PDF and its inventory only. The summary identifies the withheld companion evidence and cannot replay it. PDFs print evidence and snapshot identities and contain no links into the author's checkout. Navigation labels refer to the companion HTML package. delivery-inventory.json covers the final files and print-runtime.json records browser, Node and paper configuration. Neither an inventory nor its containing archive claims to hash itself.

    Renderer identity covers the installed Python sources, templates and contract resources, not just the package builder. Deterministic HTML, Markdown and ZIP outputs repeat byte-for-byte with identical renderer, evidence, context and verification snapshot. A new receipt check changes its recorded local check time. PDF bytes can vary with browser metadata and timestamps: retain their exact hashes and compare extracted content when testing repeat exports. No byte reproducibility claim is made for PDFs across environments.

    Review distribution permissions before sharing. Full evidence contains retained inputs and outputs; summaries omit those records but can still disclose authored context and declared model names. Private PEM key attachments are rejected, but this is not a general secret detector. Do not redact signed evidence in place. An audience-specific summary must say which content was withheld and retain its companion identity. Keep credentials and private trust-profile paths external.
